## Template and licensing

Fresh **New Age** template from [ThemeWagon](https://themewagon.com/themes/free-responsive-one-page-app-landing-bootstrap-template-download-2017/), whose download links to technext/new-age.
Source: https://github.com/technext/new-age/tree/d3d9c99d140f3d743ab384264f371c651dfc8f41
Pinned commit: `d3d9c99d140f3d743ab384264f371c651dfc8f41`.

The CSS/JS identifies Start Bootstrap New Age v6.0.1, MIT. Full upstream license from https://github.com/StartBootstrap/startbootstrap-new-age/blob/v6.0.1/LICENSE is retained in LICENSE; original source headers remain. Bootstrap 5.0.1 license is in BOOTSTRAP-LICENSE. Neither requires visible footer attribution. No license fees or paid services used.

Adaptation keeps the template's responsive navbar, split masthead, Bootstrap grid, feature/information sections and navigation behavior, with bakery copy, colors and illustration. App badges, device mockups, fake quotes and nonfunctional forms were removed. Only index.html is published; no leftover demo pages.

Original SVG illustrations by MultiMax are decorative concepts, explicitly not actual product photography. No third-party product photos used. No invented prices, reviews, addresses, hours or dietary claims. Email links open an email client; there is no checkout, payment or ordering backend.
