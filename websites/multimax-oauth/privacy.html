Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 November 2026

MultiMax Gmail Automation is a private tool operated by the owner of the connected Google account. It uses Google OAuth only to send emails that the account owner has authorized.

Google data used

  • The connected Google account’s email identity.
  • The Gmail send permission needed to create and send outgoing email.

The tool does not request permission to read, modify, or delete Gmail messages.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials are stored on infrastructure controlled by the account owner and are used only to operate the tool. MultiMax does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and control

Authorization remains until the account owner revokes access, removes the stored authorization, or stops the service. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings.
